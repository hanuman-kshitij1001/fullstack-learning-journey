Indian Railways

Indian Railways is one of the largest railway networks in the world and is operated by the Government of India. It plays a vital role in transporting passengers and freight across the country, connecting cities, towns, and remote areas.

Established in the 19th century, Indian Railways has grown into a massive organization that supports economic development and national integration.

History

The first railway line in India was opened in 1853 between Mumbai and Thane. Over time, the railway network expanded rapidly under British rule and later became a key public sector enterprise after independence.

Indian Railways is divided into multiple zones and divisions for efficient management. It employs millions of people and uses modern technology for signaling, ticketing, and train operations.

Services

Indian Railways provides various services including passenger trains, express and superfast trains, suburban railways, and freight transportation. It also operates luxury and tourist trains.
